BRIAR CHRONICLES  (version 4)
==============================

New in version 4: people can leave comments under your art.
You can read and remove them in your dashboard under "Comments",
or turn comments off there.


PUT IT ONLINE (so it has a real link)
-------------------------------------
Your site needs a host that can run server.py. GitHub Pages can't,
so we use Render. It's about $7 a month.

Step 1. Put this version on GitHub
  1. Open your repo: github.com/TheIceVirus/The-Parliament
  2. Click the old "BRIAR CHRONICLES v3" folder. Click the "..." button
     at the top right and choose "Delete directory". Commit.
  3. Back on the main page of the repo, click "Add file", then
     "Upload files".
  4. Open THIS folder on your computer, select everything inside it
     (Ctrl+A), and drag it onto the GitHub page.
     The files must sit at the top of the repo, not inside a folder.
     You should see server.py and render.yaml on the repo's main page.
  5. Click "Commit changes".

Step 2. Turn it on with Render
  1. Go to render.com and sign up with your GitHub account.
  2. Click "New", then "Blueprint".
  3. Pick The-Parliament and click "Apply".
  4. It's already set to the paid "Starter" plan. Don't switch it to
     Free. The free plan wipes your uploads and password every time
     it restarts.
  5. Wait a few minutes. Render shows your link, something like
     https://briar-chronicles.onrender.com

Step 3. Lock it
  Open your link, scroll to the bottom, click "The keeper's door",
  sign in with  sutrah  and change your password right away under
  "Profile & links". Everyone can reach the online site, so don't
  keep the starting password.

The online site starts fresh. Art you uploaded on your own computer
doesn't move over by itself. Upload it again from the online dashboard.

To update the site later: upload the new files to GitHub the same way.
Render sees the change and updates the site in a few minutes. Your art,
comments and password stay.


RUN IT ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER (optional)
--------------------------------------
  1. Close EVERY black BRIAR CHRONICLES window from older versions.
  2. Make sure this folder is unzipped (right-click the zip,
     "Extract All", then open the extracted folder).
  3. Want to keep art from an old copy? Copy the "data" folder from the
     old copy into this folder, next to server.py.
  4. Double-click  START BRIAR CHRONICLES.bat
     (On a Mac: START BRIAR CHRONICLES.command)
  5. Leave the black window open while you use it.

  This needs Python (free) from https://www.python.org/downloads/
  Tick "Add python.exe to PATH" during setup.

You should see "Version 4" at the bottom of the site and in your
dashboard. If you see an older number, an old copy is still running.
